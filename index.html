Aquatics

Swim lessons are offered year-round, from Parent/Toddler to Adult, all taught by certified American Red Cross instructors. Additional programs include water fitness, camps, lifeguarding and water safety certification, and swim teams and conditioning.

What this replaces

  • A grid of times typed into each facility page. Arroyo’s currently reads like this, and says nothing about which lanes: MondayTuesdayWednesday
    6:00am - 8:45am6:00am - 8:45am6:00am - 8:45am
    10:30am - 1:00pm10:30am - 1:00pm10:30am - 1:00pm
  • Two pools that hand over to each other. Arroyo is carrying September; the Plunge takes back over on 3 October. In the calendar that is just what the dates say, on both pools, without an announcement that has to be written and later removed.
  • The overview page and the facility page can disagree. The aquatics overview still describes Arroyo as seasonal “June to early August”, while Arroyo’s own page carries a current schedule. One calendar cannot contradict itself.
  • “Please view our Recreation Guide for a detailed listing of classes and times.” The lessons are in here too, each in its own lane.
